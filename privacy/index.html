SessionsAhead Privacy Policy

For SessionsAhead 1.0 · Effective September 20, 2026

SessionsAhead is an independent third-party companion for Calendly recurring bookings. It does not operate its own account system, advertising network, analytics SDK, or developer-hosted booking database, and it is not affiliated with or endorsed by Calendly.

Data handled by the app

To perform the actions you request, the app may handle:

  • Your Calendly account information returned by Calendly during authentication, such as account identity and timezone.
  • Client contact information you enter, including name and email address.
  • Answers you enter for Calendly booking questions.
  • Meeting information such as event type, date, time, recurrence, timezone, booking status, and Calendly booking identifiers.

How data is used

The app uses this information only to connect to your Calendly account, check availability, create the bookings you request, reconcile booking state, resume interrupted booking operations, and cancel bookings you choose to cancel.

Calendly

When you connect Calendly or create or cancel a booking, relevant information is transmitted to Calendly. Calendly may retain that information as part of your Calendly account and the bookings you create. Calendly's own privacy policy and account controls apply to data stored by Calendly.

Local storage

OAuth credentials are stored in the iOS Keychain. Series records are stored locally on your device using iOS data protection. Disconnecting Calendly removes the app's stored Calendly credentials but does not delete your local series records or records already stored by Calendly. Local app data remains on the device until it is removed by the app or the app is deleted, subject to normal operating-system backup and restore behavior.

Web services used by SessionsAhead

Firebase Hosting is used for the production HTTPS OAuth callback and Apple Associated Domains file. GitHub Pages hosts the public Privacy Policy and Support pages. These hosting providers may process ordinary technical request information required to deliver their services. SessionsAhead does not use these services for advertising, behavioral analytics, or cross-app tracking.

Tracking and advertising

SessionsAhead does not use advertising SDKs, does not sell personal data, and does not use data for cross-app or cross-site tracking.

Retention and deletion

Calendly-hosted booking and account data is managed through Calendly and is subject to Calendly's retention and deletion controls. You can disconnect Calendly in the app to remove the app's stored OAuth credentials. Deleting the app removes its local application data from the device, subject to normal operating-system backup and restore behavior.
